WetterCubePlus Europe v0.1.0-beta5

Beta version of the WetterCubePlus for Europe: weather, air quality and pollen from Open-Meteo, in English. Hardware, wiring and case are the same as for the WetterCubePlus. Please report problems as an issue.

Install the firmware

Connect the ESP32-S3 with USB-C. Chrome or Edge is required. If you tick "Erase device" in the install window, the whole flash memory is erased, including saved Wi-Fi data and settings. Without the tick the settings stay.

Which port do I choose?
After you click the install button, the browser opens a window asking to connect to a serial port. Pick your board and click "Connect":
  • Windows: an entry with "COM" and a number, for example USB-SERIAL CH340 (COM3) or USB JTAG/serial debug unit (COM5).
  • Mac: an entry with "usbmodem" or "usbserial", for example cu.usbmodem1101; best the one starting with "cu.".
  • Linux: ttyACM0 or ttyUSB0.
Not sure? Unplug the cable, open the list, plug it in again: the entry that appears is your board. If none appears, use another data cable (not a charge-only one) or another USB port.

First setup

  1. Touch calibration: tap the 4 crosses one after the other.
  2. Connect your phone or PC to the Wi-Fi “WetterCubePlus-Europe-Setup”. The password (8 digits) is shown on the display.
  3. The setup portal opens by itself (otherwise open 192.168.4.1).
  4. Choose your Wi-Fi, enter the password, the city and the country, then “Save & restart”.
  5. More settings: wettercubeplus-europe.local
