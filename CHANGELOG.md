# Changelog

## v0.1.0-beta2 (2026-10-30)

First public beta of **WetterCubePlus Europe**.

- Weather, 4-hour forecast, 24-hour curve, sun and moon, UV, air quality and pollen (4 days, with olive) from Open-Meteo / CAMS
- Notice tile calculated from the forecast: thunderstorm, heavy rain, rain, storm gusts, heat, snow or ice, frost, UV, air quality and fast pressure falls; each can be switched off in the web interface
- English interface, time zone from the location, location search with country filter, wind unit (km/h, mph, m/s, knots)
- No DWD data (no warnings, radar or Biowetter) and no official warnings
- Updates come from GitHub (this repository)
